# Reports

Standalone research reports.

| Report | Description |
|---|---|
| [The Trump-Catalyst Breakout Screener](./trump-catalyst-stock-screener.md) | A multi-dimensional, weighted methodology for screening US-listed stocks that combine strong breakout potential with a high probability of a Trump-administration policy tailwind or public mention — calibrated on the 2025 Micron (MU) and ServiceNow (NOW) re-ratings. **Educational framework, not financial advice.** |
| [Forward-Upside (Time-Staged) Screener](./forward-upside-time-staged-screener.md) | Companion report that adds the missing **time dimension** — a catalyst-lifecycle (S0–S3) model that filters out already-extended runners and ranks candidates whose move is *still ahead* (catalyst announced but not yet in earnings). **Educational framework, not financial advice.** |
