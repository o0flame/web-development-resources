## 1. Executive summary

In 2025, two very different companies — **Micron (MU)**, a memory-chip manufacturer, and
**ServiceNow (NOW)**, an enterprise-software/AI-workflow vendor — both re-rated sharply. They
share a pattern worth systematizing:

1. **They already had a strong fundamental/technical setup** (AI-driven demand, accelerating
   revenue, price strength), **and**
2. **They sat directly in the path of a Trump-administration policy priority** and received a
   concrete, *credit-claimable* government catalyst (Micron's $200B onshoring announcement made
   *jointly with the administration*; ServiceNow's GSA "OneGov" federal-AI procurement deal tied
   explicitly to Trump's AI Action Plan).

The thesis of this screener: **the biggest, most durable moves happen at the intersection of a
self-sustaining breakout setup and a high-probability political catalyst.** A stock that is
*only* a momentum name can fade; a stock that is *only* politically favored but fundamentally
weak is a headline pop that round-trips. The edge is in the **overlap.**

This document delivers:

- **A two-axis core** — *Breakout Potential* × *Trump-Catalyst Probability* — combined with a
  **geometric mean** so a candidate must score on **both** axes, not just one.
- **Four additional dimensions** you asked me to find: **Catalyst Timing & Freshness**,
  **Liquidity & Risk (hard gate)**, **Sentiment & Positioning**, and **Supply-Chain Linkage**
  to the named winners.
- A **case-study teardown** of MU and NOW that validates the rubric.
- A **step-by-step workflow**, an **illustrative candidate watchlist**, and a **risk register**.

## 2. Why this works — the mechanism

A Trump-era catalyst moves a stock through several reinforcing channels:

| Channel | What happens | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct revenue / demand** | Government becomes a customer, or policy expands the end-market | GSA OneGov deal → ServiceNow federal ACV +30% YoY |
| **Protected margin** | Tariffs / "build-in-USA" exemptions shield domestic producers | Aug 2025 ~100% chip-tariff threat exempting US fabs → Micron |
| **Narrative / multiple expansion** | Stock becomes the "face" of a Trump priority; multiple re-rates | Stargate ($500B) → AI-infrastructure complex |
| **Reflexive flows** | Headlines pull in momentum and retail; positioning amplifies | Round-number capex pledges, White House podium moments |

The framework scores a candidate's exposure to **each** channel, because a stock hitting several
channels at once (Micron hit demand + protected margin + narrative) produces the strongest,
most durable re-rating.

## 3. Case-study teardown — calibrating the model on MU and NOW

### 3.1 Micron (MU) — the "protected reshoring + AI-demand" archetype

**Breakout side (fundamentals/technical):**
- HBM (high-bandwidth memory) demand from the AI buildout: by FY25 Micron held **~21% of the
  global HBM market**, with HBM capacity reportedly **sold out through end-2026** and gross
  margins pushed toward **~70%**.
- Record fundamentals: Fiscal Q3 (reported 25 Jun 2025) **record $9.30B revenue**, HBM up ~50%
  sequentially, data-center revenue doubled YoY.
- Price: MU roughly **tripled across 2024–2025** on the HBM/AI cycle.

**Trump-catalyst side:**
- **$200B US investment announced *jointly with the Trump administration* (June 2025)** —
  ~$150B manufacturing + ~$50B R&D, ~90,000 jobs, a second Idaho fab, additional New York
  (Clay) fabs, and modernization of Manassas, VA to onshore advanced DRAM from Taiwan. The
  White House framed it as a win for the onshoring/"America-first" agenda.
- **CHIPS funding finalized under Trump:** up to ~$6.165B direct funding; the NY final funding
  agreement signed 11 Jun 2025; ~$275M for Manassas.
- **Section 232 / ~100% semiconductor tariff threat (6 Aug 2025)**, explicitly exempting
  companies *building in the United States* — a direct structural tailwind for the only US-based
  advanced-memory maker.
- **Stargate (announced by Trump at the White House, 21 Jan 2025)** institutionalized the HBM
  demand wave.

**Why it scores high on the model:** strong on *every* breakout sub-factor **and** hits 3–4
Trump channels (demand via Stargate, protected margin via tariffs, narrative via onshoring,
direct government endorsement). This is the template.

> *Caveat surfaced in research:* a clean same-day MU price spike tied to the June 2025
> announcement could not be isolated (it coincided with the earnings window). A widely
> circulated May 2026 rally-day "+20%" story sits **outside** the 2025 window and is
> single-source — excluded from calibration.

### 3.2 ServiceNow (NOW) — the "federal-AI procurement" archetype

**Breakout side (fundamentals/technical):**
- **Now Assist / agentic-AI** momentum: Q2 FY25 subscription revenue **$3.11B (+22.5% YoY)**;
  Q3 FY25 **$3.30B (+21.5% YoY)**, with Now Assist ACV on pace to exceed **$500M** and
  consumption up sharply.
- Durable ~20%+ subscription growth with high net-retention — a Rule-of-40 grower.

**Trump-catalyst side:**
- **GSA "OneGov" deal (3 Sep 2025)** — federal agencies get up to **70% off** an ITSM Pro
  bundle through Sept 2028, bundling ServiceNow's agentic-AI platform. GSA explicitly tied it
  to **Trump's AI Action Plan** and a procurement-consolidation executive order. **NOW rose
  ~3.3% intraday**; sell-side reiterated Buy.
- **Trump "AI Action Plan" — "Winning the Race" (23 Jul 2025):** a pillar of *rapid federal
  adoption of AI* — a structural tailwind for enterprise workflow/SaaS vendors.
- **U.S. Tech Force founding partner** (OPM) — ~1,000 specialists embedded in agencies for AI
  modernization, alongside Microsoft, Oracle, Palantir, Salesforce.
- Result: **U.S. Federal net-new ACV grew >30% YoY** in Q3.

**Why it scores well but lower than MU on the catalyst axis:** the catalyst is real and
credit-claimable (government as a *direct customer*, policy-aligned), but ServiceNow benefited
through **one** primary channel (direct revenue) rather than MU's three. There was **no**
verified personal Trump–McDermott relationship or Trump naming the company. (A "Trump's
ServiceNow stake" headline circulated but is **unverified and treated as noise.**) The bulk of
NOW's 2025 gain was fundamentals-led, with the policy deal as an accelerant — a useful reminder
that the *breakout axis* often does the heavy lifting and the *catalyst axis* is the amplifier.

**Calibration takeaway:** MU ≈ a 90+ composite (both axes maxed, multi-channel); NOW ≈ a
high-70s/low-80s composite (breakout strong, catalyst strong-but-single-channel). The rubric in
§6 is tuned to reproduce that ordering.

## 5. What actually triggers a positive Trump mention (the catalyst-probability features)

Distilled from 2025 events — Apple's **$500B→$600B** US pledge, **TSMC's +$100B Arizona**
($165B total), **Stargate** ($500B), **Nvidia/Jensen Huang** ("one of the most brilliant men"),
**US Steel/Nippon** (golden share), pharma & auto reshoring pledges. The strongest mentions hit
**4+** of these at once:

1. **A huge, round-number US capex/jobs pledge** ($100B / $500B; "100,000 jobs"). Headline
   friendliness beats specificity.
2. **Reshoring framing** — physical US factories/fabs, ideally in a **swing or industrial
   state** (Arizona, Ohio, Pennsylvania, New York/Clay).
3. **Tariff leverage** — the pledge is offered as an *escape from* tariffs, letting Trump claim
   personal credit.
4. **AI / chip / "American dominance" alignment** — the hottest sector; gets top billing.
5. **CEO relationship & physical proximity** — Mar-a-Lago, inauguration front row, Davos panel,
   a White House podium appearance beside Trump.
6. **"Credit-claimable / American greatness" narrative** — framed as secured "under President
   Trump," "historic," "largest ever."
7. **Reciprocity/leverage to the government** — golden shares, MFN deals, export approvals,
   government contracts.
8. **A sector Trump personally watches** — chips, autos, steel, pharma, Big Tech, AI.

> *Fact-check flags from research:* many auto "investment" figures were **reallocations**, not
> net-new plants (PolitiFact); SoftBank's pledge delivery is historically unreliable; pledge
> totals are **announcements, not audited spend.** Weight *announced* pledges accordingly.

## 6. The scoring algorithm

### 6.1 Structure

Six dimensions. The **two core axes** are combined with a **geometric mean** (so both must be
present); the four supporting dimensions act as **multipliers, bonuses, or a hard gate.**

```
Core      = sqrt( BP × TC )                 # both axes required
Adjusted  = Core × Timing × Sentiment + SupplyChainBonus + DisclosureBonus
Final     = Adjusted × LiquidityRiskGate    # gate ∈ {0, 1}; 0 = disqualified
                                            # (and apply PunishedSector veto → 0)
```

Each of **BP** and **TC** is 0–100. `Timing` and `Sentiment` are multipliers in **[0.8, 1.2]**.
`SupplyChainBonus` and `DisclosureBonus` are each **0–10**. `LiquidityRiskGate` is a pass/fail
**{0,1}**. A stock in a **punished sector** (theme #9) is vetoed to 0 regardless of other scores.

### 6.2 Dimension A — Breakout / Upside Potential (BP, 0–100)

Sub-factor weights sum to 100. Score each 0–100, then weight.

| Sub-factor | Weight | Measurable criteria / thresholds |
|---|---:|---|
| **Earnings-revision momentum** | 20% | Zacks Rank **1–2**; net upward EPS revisions over 30–90d; positive consensus revision **>5–10%**; recent positive earnings surprise |
| **Relative strength / trend** | 25% | IBD **RS ≥ 80** (prefer 80–90+); Minervini Trend Template (price > 50/150/200-day SMAs, 50>150>200, 200-day rising); within **15% of 52-wk high**, breaking a base |
| **Institutional accumulation** | 15% | IBD A/D rating **A or B**; Up/Down Volume Ratio **≥ 1.3**; breakout volume **≥ 40–50%** above 50-day avg; rising OBV |
| **Secular demand / growth** | 20% | Revenue growth **≥ 20–25% YoY**; large/expanding TAM; **Rule of 40** (growth% + FCF margin ≥ 40) for software |
| **Valuation runway** | 10% | **PEG ≈ 1** (penalize >2); forward P/E vs growth; EV/Sales reasonable for the growth tier |
| **Squeeze/float fuel** *(accelerant)* | 10% | Short float **>15%** (high-conviction >20%), days-to-cover **>5**; small float; negative dealer GEX with call OI just above spot |

### 6.3 Dimension B — Trump-Catalyst Probability (TC, 0–100)

| Sub-factor | Weight | Measurable criteria |
|---|---:|---|
| **Thematic alignment** | 30% | Membership in a **confirmed** theme (§4 #1–7). Full marks for AI/semis/energy-for-AI/defense/crypto; partial for adjacent |
| **US capex/jobs pledge** | 20% | Announced **round-number** US investment / jobs; bonus if in a swing/industrial state; scale by size & recency |
| **Govt revenue / contracts** | 15% | Federal procurement exposure (GSA OneGov-type), defense backlog, Tech-Force/contract status |
| **CEO relationship & visibility** | 15% | Documented White House podium / Mar-a-Lago / Davos / inauguration presence; public praise |
| **Credit-claimable narrative** | 10% | "Historic / largest-ever / under President Trump" framing available |
| **Tariff-shield / reshoring leverage** | 10% | Company *benefits* from tariffs (builds in US / domestic producer), not harmed by them |
| **🚫 Punished-sector veto** | — | If in theme #9 (solar/wind/EV-credit-dependent/China-import-exposed) → **TC capped low and Final = 0** |

### 6.4 Supporting dimensions

- **Timing & Freshness (× 0.8–1.2):** Is a catalyst *window* near and *not yet priced*? Upcoming
  earnings, a scheduled policy event/EO, a pending tariff decision, an investor day. **1.2** =
  fresh, imminent, under-the-radar catalyst; **1.0** = neutral; **0.8** = catalyst already old/
  fully priced. *Avoid buying the announcement after the gap.*
- **Sentiment & Positioning (× 0.8–1.2):** Room to run vs. over-owned/over-hyped. **1.2** = under-
  owned, improving sell-side, not yet a crowded retail name; **0.8** = euphoric, parabolic,
  heavily crowded (mean-reversion risk).
- **Supply-Chain Linkage (+0 to +10):** Second-derivative exposure to a named winner — HBM/
  packaging/equipment suppliers to Micron, data-center power/cooling for Stargate, ServiceNow
  ecosystem, defense primes' subs. Rewards the "pick-and-shovel" names that ride a confirmed
  leader's wave.
- **Disclosed Politically-Connected Accumulation — DPA (+0 to +10):** A *confirmation* overlay
  from **public** financial-disclosure filings (STOCK Act periodic transaction reports of members
  of Congress / senior officials, and similar mandated disclosures). When a cluster of
  politically-connected insiders is *disclosed* buying a name, it corroborates that the
  policy-tailwind + conviction overlap is real. Scoring: **+10** = a large, recent, *discretionary*
  disclosed buy or new position in a name that already scores well on BP×TC; **+5** = a smaller
  disclosed buy; **0** = "non-discretionary"/managed-account/blind-trust trades (no conviction
  credit); **negative flag** = disclosed *net selling* (treat as a yellow flag — trim the
  Sentiment multiplier). See §8.1 for a worked example. *Caveats are serious — read §9.*
- **Liquidity & Risk Gate ({0,1} — hard pass/fail):** Require a minimum to avoid illiquid pump
  traps: e.g., **market cap ≥ $2B**, **ADV ≥ $20M/day**, tight spreads, no going-concern/dilution
  red flags, no active fraud/SEC action. Fail → **Final = 0.** This dimension is a *safety
  filter*, never a reason to buy.

### 6.5 Conviction tiers

| Final score | Tier | Interpretation |
|---:|---|---|
| **80–100** | High-conviction watch | Both axes strong + fresh catalyst + clean liquidity |
| **65–79** | Active watch | Strong but one axis or timing softer |
| **50–64** | Monitor | One axis carrying it; needs a catalyst to develop |
| **< 50** | Pass | Insufficient overlap |

### 6.6 Sanity check against the case studies

- **MU:** BP ≈ 92 (every sub-factor strong), TC ≈ 95 (multi-channel: thematic + pledge + tariff
  shield + endorsement). Core = √(92×95) ≈ **93**; Timing ~1.0, Sentiment ~0.95 (already extended),
  SupplyChain +0 → **~89, High-conviction.** ✅ Matches reality.
- **NOW:** BP ≈ 85, TC ≈ 72 (single channel: direct federal revenue; no CEO/personal angle).
  Core = √(85×72) ≈ **78**; Timing ~1.0, Sentiment ~1.0 → **~78, Active watch.** ✅ Matches the
  "fundamentals-led with policy accelerant" read.

The geometric mean is what keeps a one-axis name (e.g., a politically favored but fundamentally
weak SMR, or a strong momentum name with zero policy angle) **out** of the top tier.

## 7. Step-by-step screening workflow

1. **Universe & theme filter.** Start from US-listed names **inside confirmed themes** (§4 #1–7).
   Immediately **drop punished-sector** names (#9).
2. **Liquidity/Risk gate.** Apply the §6.4 hard gate (cap, ADV, spreads, no fraud/dilution flags).
   Survivors only.
3. **Score Breakout Potential (BP).** Pull Zacks Rank, RS rating, trend-template checklist,
   A/D rating, up/down volume, revenue growth / Rule-of-40, PEG/EV-Sales, short float & DTC.
4. **Score Trump-Catalyst Probability (TC).** Map each name against the 8 mention-triggers (§5)
   and its policy channels (§2). Credit *confirmed/announced* catalysts; discount rumor.
5. **Compute Core** = √(BP×TC). Drop anything with Core < 60 or with either axis < 50.
6. **Apply Timing, Sentiment, Supply-Chain.** Favor **fresh, not-yet-priced** catalysts and
   **under-owned** setups; add the pick-and-shovel bonus.
7. **Rank by Final score → tiers.** Build a watchlist, not a portfolio.
8. **Define the trade plan *before* entry** (see §9 risk): entry trigger (e.g., base breakout on
   volume), invalidation/stop, position size, and a catalyst-failure exit. **Never** chase the
   post-headline gap.
9. **Re-score on every new data point** (earnings, EO, tariff ruling, pledge). Scores decay; a
   catalyst that fully prices in drops Timing toward 0.8.

## 9. Risk register, false-signal traps & ethics

**Market/strategy risks**
- **Buy-the-rumor, sell-the-news:** the catalyst is often *priced in* by the time it's a
  headline. The Timing multiplier exists to fight this; discipline must enforce it.
- **Reversal risk:** political favor is fickle — a tariff carve-out, an export ban, or a feud can
  flip a tailwind to a headwind overnight.
- **Announcement ≠ delivery:** many 2025 "investment" pledges were reallocations or unaudited
  (auto figures fact-checked as exaggerated; SoftBank's delivery history is poor). Weight
  *announced* numbers skeptically.
- **Crowding / valuation:** the most obvious names (NVDA, PLTR) carry mean-reversion risk after
  parabolic runs. High rubric score ≠ good entry price.
- **Speculative blow-up risk:** pre-revenue SMRs and crypto proxies are sentiment-driven and can
  draw down 50%+ fast.
- **Single-source/“noise” catalysts:** discard unverified items (e.g., the unconfirmed "Trump's
  ServiceNow stake" headline). Require *credible, primary* confirmation (White House/GSA/SEC).

**Disclosure-overlay (DPA) risks — read before using §8.1**
- **Reporting lag:** STOCK Act periodic transaction reports can be filed up to ~30–45 days after
  the trade. By the time you see it, the move may be done — you are *late*, not early.
- **Broad ranges:** disclosures report **bands** (e.g., "$1M–5M"), not exact size, price, or
  remaining position. You cannot infer conviction precisely.
- **Non-discretionary / managed accounts:** trades flagged "non-discretionary" (or in a blind
  trust) are **not** the filer's conviction — give them **zero** DPA credit (e.g., Intel above).
- **Provenance:** confirm *whose* disclosure it is from a primary source before trusting it; a
  watermarked screenshot is not verification.
- **Clustering ≠ causation, and ≠ legality of mirroring being a guarantee of returns.** Many
  disclosed "buys" sit in managed index-like accounts and carry no signal at all.

**Process safeguards**
- Pre-define entry, **stop/invalidation**, size, and a **catalyst-failure exit** before entering.
- Treat the watchlist as candidates to *study*, never an instruction to buy.
- Diversify; never bet the book on one political headline.

**Ethics & legality**
- **Public information only.** This framework uses policy actions, filings, and price/volume —
  never material non-public information. Trading on MNPI is a crime.
- **No front-running of government action** based on non-public knowledge. If you have access to
  non-public government or corporate information, this framework does **not** apply to you.
- **Mirroring *disclosed* trades is legal** — STOCK Act / official financial disclosures are
  public records, and trackers (Quiver Quantitative, Unusual Whales, Capitol Trades) aggregate
  them openly. Using that public data is fine; it is still **not advice** and carries the lag/
  band/managed-account caveats in §9.
